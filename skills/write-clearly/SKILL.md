# Write Clearly

Help the intended reader understand or act without removing information they need.
Use this for substantive drafting or revision, not as a mandatory pass over every
answer. Preserve the user's requested voice, creative choices, audience, and format.

## Establish what the text must carry

Read the task and source material. Identify the reader, purpose, requested form,
and facts or instructions that must survive. Preserve numbers, links, domain terms,
qualifiers, authority, negation, and uncertainty. Do not invent first-person
experience, evidence, certainty, or promises to sound authentic. A writing request
is not permission to change product rules or reconcile code and documentation.
If sources conflict or meaning is unknown, retain that limit or ask a focused
question when it prevents a faithful draft; do not silently resolve it.

## Draft or revise

Use [voice guidance](references/voice.md) for plain prose, terminology distinctions,
and before/after examples. Name the actor, action, and relevant condition. Replace
filler or vague qualifications with the fact, limitation, or decision they conceal.
Keep one term for one concept within its context; preserve established domain
vocabulary where a plainer substitute would change meaning.

Each sentence should earn its place through information, explanation, or a needed
connection. Brevity must preserve meaning and readability. Keep repetition needed
for comprehension rather than forcing density or compressing away information.
Use structure that helps the reader and honors the request; do not impose heading,
list, or format bans. Requested creative voice takes precedence over the default.

## Check fidelity

Compare the draft with the source and task. Check that conditions, distinctions,
claims, and permissions remain intact. Return the requested text; explain a
meaning-changing ambiguity or a requested alteration when the reader needs it.
Adapt tone without changing an evidence-based conclusion to reassure or agree
with the user. Preserve requested fiction, creative voice, and preferences;
this fidelity check does not turn a prose edit into factual research.
Do not add a compulsory editing report. Improving prose does not validate its
facts or establish a measured improvement in readability or task outcomes.
